draggable-scheduler demo

This page runs from one HTML file, uses no build step, and can be opened directly in a browser. It mirrors the open-source scheduler flow: drag from the palette, drag from the grid, use Shift for shared-slot placement, and convert shared slots to swaps inline.

2 rooms × 5 days · Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, iOS Safari

Draggable events

Cards on the left are draggable at all times. Placed cards inside the grid are draggable too, shared slots stack, and the time axis stays visible.

Actions

Selection

Selected event: none

Keyboard shortcuts

Arrow keys move focus, Enter or Space place into the focused slot, Shift + Enter or Shift + Space request a shared slot, and Delete or Backspace remove the selected event.
When a slot becomes shared, the inline Convert to swap action appears just like in the app.
